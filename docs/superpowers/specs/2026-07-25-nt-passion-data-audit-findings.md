## Finding 1: Pilate's cross inscription is said to be in "Hebrew," but the ESV's primary text says "Aramaic"
- **Category:** Incorrect
- **Verse(s):** John 19:19-20
- **Current DB state:** `pilate`'s description reads "Posted a sign above the cross: 'Jesus of Nazareth, King of the Jews' in Hebrew, Latin, and Greek."
- **Proposed correction:** John 19:20 (ESV) reads "...it was written in Aramaic, in Latin, and in Greek" — the ESV's main text renders the Greek word Ἑβραϊστί as "Aramaic" here (reflecting the vernacular actually spoken in first-century Judea), with a footnote offering "Or Hebrew" as an alternate. Since this project's source of truth is the ESV's primary text, change "in Hebrew, Latin, and Greek" to "in Aramaic, Latin, and Greek."
- **Severity:** Important

## Finding 2: Herod Agrippa I's death quote drops "Immediately" and the causal clause, and substitutes "died" for "breathed his last"
- **Category:** Incorrect
- **Verse(s):** Acts 12:23
- **Current DB state:** `herod_agrippa1`'s description quotes Acts 12:23 as "'An angel of the Lord struck him down, and he was eaten by worms and died.'"
- **Proposed correction:** The ESV's actual wording is "Immediately an angel of the Lord struck him down, because he did not give God the glory, and he was eaten by worms and breathed his last." The current text drops the opening "Immediately," silently omits the causal clause "because he did not give God the glory" (the verse's actual theological point — why he was struck down) without any ellipsis marking the gap, and changes "breathed his last" to "died." Replace with: "'Immediately an angel of the Lord struck him down, because he did not give God the glory, and he was eaten by worms and breathed his last.'"
- **Severity:** Important
