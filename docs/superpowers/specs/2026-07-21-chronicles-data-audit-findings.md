## Finding 2: Jeduthun's `ally_of` relationships with Asaph and Heman cite "1 Chr 15:17–19" as a source, but Jeduthun is not named anywhere in that verse range — the text names "Ethan" there

- **Category:** Unsupported
- **Verse(s):** 1 Chronicles 15:17-19 (ESV, live-fetched, verbatim): "So the Levites appointed Heman the son of Joel; and of his brothers Asaph the son of Berechiah; and of the sons of Merari, their brothers, Ethan the son of Kushaiah... The singers, Heman, Asaph, and Ethan, were to sound bronze cymbals." **Jeduthun's name does not appear in verses 17-19.** The three named cymbal-players are Heman, Asaph, and Ethan (son of Kushaiah) — not Jeduthun.
- **Current DB state:** `scripts/seed-chronicles.ts` line 254: `insertRel("asaph", "ally_of", "jeduthun", "Colleagues in the temple music ministry (1 Chr 15:17–19)")`, and line 253's `asaph`/`heman` relationship additionally cites the same range for the Asaph-Heman pairing (that one is fine, since both Asaph and Heman ARE named in 15:17-19). The `jeduthun` person record itself already carries an appropriately-hedged `alsoKnownAs: "...Ethan the Ezrahite"` and description ("Likely the same person as Ethan the Ezrahite") — so the file elsewhere treats Ethan-in-15:19 as the same individual as Jeduthun by editorial choice. That identification is a reasonable, commonly-held inference (supported by 1 Chr 16:37-42, where Jeduthun fills the exact parallel role Ethan filled in ch. 15, and by the Psalm 39/62/77 headers), but it is never stated explicitly anywhere in Chronicles — no verse says "Ethan, that is, Jeduthun." Citing "1 Chr 15:17–19" as direct textual support for a relationship keyed to the literal name "Jeduthun" therefore overstates what that specific verse range says.
- **Proposed correction:** Either (a) change the cited range for these two relationships to 1 Chr 16:41-42 (where Jeduthun is named directly, alongside Heman, "to give thanks to the Lord" — Asaph is mentioned earlier in the same chapter as being left "before the ark," so 16:37-42 covers all three), or (b) keep 1 Chr 15:17-19 but amend the note to read "Colleagues in the temple music ministry (1 Chr 15:17–19, as Ethan; cf. 16:41–42)" to make the identification explicit rather than implicit. Recommend (a) for the cleanest fix, since 16:41-42 already appears as the ref citation for Jeduthun elsewhere in the same file (line 295) and directly names him without requiring the Ethan-identification inference.
- **Severity:** Minor — the underlying relationship (Jeduthun/Ethan was a colleague of Asaph and Heman) is true and well-supported overall by the file's own broader citation pattern; only this specific verse-range citation for these two relationship rows is imprecise.

## Finding 3: Jehoram of Judah's marriage to Athaliah is correctly sourced, but the file never records that Athaliah is elsewhere called "granddaughter of Omri" rather than "daughter of Ahab" — worth a note for the controller, not a correction

- **Category:** Unsupported
- **Verse(s):** 2 Chronicles 21:6 (ESV, live-fetched, verbatim): "...for the daughter of Ahab was his wife." This matches the DB's citation exactly. However, 2 Kings 8:26 and 2 Chronicles 22:2 (both ESV, live-fetched, verbatim) independently state: "His mother's name was Athaliah; she was a granddaughter of Omri king of Israel" / "His mother's name was Athaliah, the granddaughter of Omri." No single verse explicitly states "Athaliah, daughter of Ahab" by name in one clause — the "daughter of Ahab" wording (2 Chr 21:6, and its parallel 2 Kgs 8:18) never names her, and the verses that name her ("Athaliah") call her "granddaughter of Omri," not "daughter of Ahab."
- **Current DB state:** `scripts/seed-chronicles.ts` line 245: `insertRel("jehoram_judah", "spouse_of", "athaliah", "Jehoram married Athaliah daughter of Ahab (2 Chr 21:6)")`.
- **Why this is flagged despite being a defensible, standard harmonization:** The identification of the unnamed "daughter of Ahab" (2 Chr 21:6) with the separately-named "Athaliah, granddaughter of Omri" (2 Kgs 8:26) as the same person is the universally accepted reading (she is Ahaziah's mother in both traditions, and "daughter of Ahab" is understood as loose genealogical language for "female descendant of Ahab's house," consistent with Hebrew usage elsewhere) — so the relationship itself is not wrong. But the note's specific wording, "Athaliah daughter of Ahab," synthesizes a naming construction ("[name] daughter of [Ahab]") that doesn't appear together verbatim in any single cited verse, and the verses that do name her call her Omri's granddaughter, one generation further removed than "daughter of Ahab" would suggest if read literally.
- **Proposed correction:** No change strictly required — this is standard biblical harmonization also used correctly elsewhere in the audited series. Optionally, for precision, the note could read "Jehoram married Athaliah, called 'daughter of Ahab' in 2 Chr 21:6 and 'granddaughter of Omri' in 2 Kgs 8:26/2 Chr 22:2" to reflect that the file is bridging two verses rather than quoting one. Flagging per the brief's instruction to include borderline observations for the controller's judgment rather than silently omit them.
- **Severity:** Minor — textually defensible as-is; purely a precision/citation-completeness suggestion.
