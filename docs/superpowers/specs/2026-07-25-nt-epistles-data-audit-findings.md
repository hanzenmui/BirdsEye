## Finding 2: Andronicus's and Junia's quote — "outstanding among the apostles" — doesn't match the ESV's actual wording at Romans 16:7
- **Category:** Incorrect
- **Verse(s):** Romans 16:7
- **Current DB state:** Four locations quote "outstanding among the apostles": `andronicus`'s description, `junia`'s description, and both people's Romans 16:7 scripture_refs notes.
- **Proposed correction:** Romans 16:7 (ESV) reads "Greet Andronicus and Junia, my kinsmen and my fellow prisoners. They are well known to the apostles, and they were in Christ before me." The ESV's actual wording is "well known to the apostles" — a translation choice that, unlike "outstanding among the apostles" (found in the NIV, NRSV, and others), implies Andronicus and Junia were well-regarded *by* the apostles rather than *being* apostles themselves. This is one of the most-discussed translation cruxes in the NT regarding female apostleship, and the two readings carry different implications. Replace "outstanding among the apostles" with "well known to the apostles" in all four locations to match the ESV. Separately (not a quote-fidelity issue, but worth the controller's attention): `junia`'s "apostle" tag and "Likely a female apostle" claim rest specifically on the "outstanding among" reading — with the ESV's actual wording, that claim is less directly supported by this file's own cited source and is a judgment call rather than a clear-cut correction, since the "among the apostles" reading remains a live scholarly position even though it isn't the ESV's choice.
- **Severity:** Important
