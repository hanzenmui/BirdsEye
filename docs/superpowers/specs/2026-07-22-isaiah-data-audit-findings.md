## Finding 1: Maher-shalal-hash-baz's description quotes a name-meaning gloss that is not the ESV's wording

- **Category:** Incorrect
- **Verse(s):** Isaiah 8:1 (ESV, live-fetched, verbatim, cross-confirmed via three independent fetches of esv.org and a third-party ESV mirror): "Take a large tablet and write on it in common characters, 'Belonging to Maher-shalal-hash-baz.'" ESV footnote on this verse: "*Maher-shalal-hash-baz* means *The spoil speeds, the prey hastens*."
- **Current DB state:** `scripts/seed-isaiah.ts` line 151, `maher_shalal.description`: "Isaiah's second son, whose name means **'Swift is the booty, speedy is the prey'**. Born as a prophetic sign of the imminent fall of Damascus and Samaria (Isaiah 8:1–4)."
- **Why this is flagged:** The description presents this as a specific quoted gloss of the name's meaning, but the ESV's own footnote translates it as "The spoil speeds, the prey hastens" — not "Swift is the booty, speedy is the prey." The two phrasings are close in sense (both describe a swift plundering) but are not the same wording, and this series' established source-of-truth convention is the ESV specifically. "Swift is the booty, speedy is the prey" reads as a paraphrase closer to other translations' renderings, not the ESV's. This is precisely the kind of name-meaning-gloss check the task brief called out for special attention.
- **Proposed correction:** Replace the quoted phrase with the ESV's actual footnote wording, e.g.: "Isaiah's second son, whose name means 'The spoil speeds, the prey hastens' (Isaiah 8:1 ESV footnote). Born as a prophetic sign of the imminent fall of Damascus and Samaria (Isaiah 8:1–4)."
- **Severity:** Important — a quoted translation gloss that doesn't match the cited source is a specific, verifiable factual error, but it's a single self-contained description-field correction with no cascading structural effect, consistent with this series' precedent of reserving Critical for structural gaps.

## Finding 2: The Isaiah 7:1–12 ref on Ahaz is captioned as covering "the Immanuel sign," but the Immanuel sign itself (the naming, v. 14) falls outside that verse range

- **Category:** Incorrect
- **Verse(s):** Isaiah 7:10–14 (ESV, live-fetched, verbatim, cross-confirmed via two independent fetches): v.10 "Again the Lord spoke to Ahaz:" v.11 "Ask a sign of the Lord your God; let it be deep as Sheol or high as heaven." v.12 "But Ahaz said, 'I will not ask, and I will not put the Lord to the test.'" v.13 "And he said, 'Hear then, O house of David!...'" **v.14 "Therefore the Lord himself will give you a sign. Behold, the virgin shall conceive and bear a son, and shall call his name Immanuel."**
- **Current DB state:** `scripts/seed-isaiah.ts` line 188: `insertRef("ahaz_judah", "Isaiah", 7, 1, 7, 12, "Isaiah meets Ahaz with the Immanuel sign")` — chapter/verse range is 7:1 through 7:12.
- **Why this is flagged:** Verses 1–12 cover the historical setup (the Syro-Ephraimite threat), Isaiah being sent to meet Ahaz (v. 3), God's offer of a sign through Isaiah (vv. 10–11), and Ahaz's refusal (v. 12). The Immanuel sign itself — "Behold, the virgin shall conceive... shall call his name Immanuel" — is given in verse 14, one verse past the ref's stated end. The note text says the ref is about "the Immanuel sign," but the actual sign-naming verse (14) isn't included in the range, so the ref's range and its own note are mismatched.
- **Proposed correction:** Extend the ref's `chapter_end`/`verse_end` from 7:12 to at least 7:14 (through the naming of Immanuel), e.g. `7:1–7:14`, so the range actually contains the verse the note describes. (Extending further to 7:17, where the oracle's near-term timeframe is given, is a defensible alternative but not necessary to fix the core mismatch — 7:14 is the minimum needed for the note to match the range.)
- **Severity:** Important — a ref range/note-text mismatch on a single existing ref row; no missing person or relationship, no cascading effect.

## Finding 3: Hezekiah — named in the same Isaiah 1:1 superscription as Uzziah, Jotham, and Ahaz — has no scripture_refs row anywhere in the codebase pointing at Isaiah 1:1

- **Category:** Missing
- **Verse(s):** Isaiah 1:1 (ESV, live-fetched, verbatim): "The vision of Isaiah the son of Amoz, which he saw concerning Judah and Jerusalem in the days of Uzziah, Jotham, Ahaz, and Hezekiah, kings of Judah." All four kings are named together in one sentence.
- **Current DB state:** `scripts/seed-isaiah.ts` gives `uzziah`, `jotham_judah`, and `ahaz_judah` each their own Isaiah 1:1 ref (lines 184, 186, 187: "One of the kings during Isaiah's ministry"). Hezekiah is loaded into this file via `loadExisting` (line 84) and is used in two relationships (`ahaz_judah parent_of Hezekiah`, `isaiah ally_of hezekiah`), but no `insertRef` call in this file — or, per grep, in `scripts/seed-2kings.ts` (Hezekiah's own originating seed file, which gives him only a `2 Kings 18:1–20:21` ref) — points Hezekiah at Isaiah 1:1.
- **Why this is flagged:** This is presented non-prescriptively, per the brief's guidance for borderline items with no single clearly-correct fix. On one hand, the four-kings superscription is a natural, symmetric case for giving all four people (not just three) a ref to the verse that names them together — the current state means 3 of the 4 kings named in Isaiah 1:1 have a ref there and the fourth doesn't, which reads as an asymmetric gap once you notice it. On the other hand, Hezekiah's own scripture-ref set is arguably out of scope for this file to touch (his record and refs originate in `seed-2kings.ts`), and one could reasonably decide this ref belongs there instead, or that it's simply not necessary since Hezekiah already has extensive Isaiah-related coverage via the `isaiah ally_of hezekiah` relationship's note (which cites "2 Kgs 19–20; Isaiah 36–39"). Options: (a) add `insertRef("hezekiah", "Isaiah", 1, 1, 1, 1, "One of the kings during Isaiah's ministry")` for symmetry with the other three kings; (b) leave as-is on the reasoning that Hezekiah's ref set is scoped to his own originating file; (c) address it in Hezekiah's own file instead of here. Flagging for the review step to resolve.
- **Severity:** Minor — this is a coverage-symmetry observation about one missing ref row for an already-well-represented person, not a missing person/relationship with cascading effects.
