## Finding 2: Thomas's quote reorders words and substitutes "not" for "never"
- **Category:** Incorrect
- **Verse(s):** John 20:25
- **Current DB state:** `thomas`'s description quotes him as saying "Unless I see the mark of the nails in his hands…I will not believe."
- **Proposed correction:** The ESV's actual wording is "Unless I see in his hands the mark of the nails, and place my finger into the mark of the nails, and place my hand into his side, I will never believe." The current text reorders "the mark of the nails in his hands" (ESV: "in his hands the mark of the nails") and ends with "I will not believe" where the ESV says "I will never believe." The ellipsis correctly marks the omitted middle clause, but the retained portions should match the ESV exactly: "Unless I see in his hands the mark of the nails…I will never believe."
- **Severity:** Important
