## Finding 1: Daniel's description misattributes the handwriting-on-the-wall to Daniel himself
- **Category:** Incorrect
- **Verse(s):** Daniel 5:5, 5:24-28
- **Current DB state:** Daniel's `description` reads "...He interpreted Nebuchadnezzar's statue dream and wrote on the wall that appeared at Belshazzar's feast." — grammatically this attributes the writing action ("wrote on the wall") to Daniel.
- **Proposed correction:** Reword to "...He interpreted Nebuchadnezzar's statue dream and read and interpreted the mysterious writing that appeared on the wall at Belshazzar's feast." The text is explicit that a disembodied hand did the writing ("the fingers of a human hand appeared and wrote on the plaster of the wall," Dan 5:5); Daniel's role was to read and interpret it (Dan 5:24-28), not to write it.
- **Severity:** Important

## Finding 2: Nebuchadnezzar's Daniel ref omits his prominent role in Daniel 3
